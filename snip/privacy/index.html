Snip / Privacy

The short version

Snip keeps everything on your Mac. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no telemetry. Nothing you type, copy, or store ever leaves your machine.

Your snippets

Your snippet library is stored locally as a plain JSON file at ~/Library/Application Support/Snip/. Snip never uploads, syncs, or transmits it anywhere.

Accessibility permission

Snip requires macOS's Accessibility permission for two things: noticing your trigger (a low-level event tap watches for the held button or shortcut) and inserting text (a synthesized paste into the app you're using). Snip does not read your screen, log your keystrokes, or observe anything beyond the trigger it is configured to watch for.

Network

The only network request Snip makes is an update check: it fetches a small appcast.xml file from Snip's public GitHub repository so it can tell you when a new version is available. No identifying information is sent with that request. You can turn automatic checks off in Settings.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here.
